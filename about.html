About Ringly

What's Ringly

Ringly (App offered by Voxstream Technologies Private Limited) is a social platform that builds real friendships through matchmaker-led audio conversations - no photos, only AI avatars.It brings people together inside safe, moderated communities where voice and personality matter more than looks. The experience is monetized through virtual gifting and premium interactions, creating authentic, high-trust engagement at scale.

Who is behind Ringly

Ringly is an app by Voxstream Technologies Private Limited, based in Delhi, India. Grievance and support contact: support@ringly.in.

What you can do on Ringly

  • Connect over shared hobbies — talk 1:1 with people who love the same things you do.
  • Talk to experts — get a direct conversation with someone who knows their field.
  • Build real friendships — matchmaker-led audio conversations, with voice and personality first.
  • Stay private — AI avatars instead of photos, and AI-moderated calls.
